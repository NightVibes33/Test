## PumpkinMode computation: what is actually known

Confirmed: a new external MobileGestalt getter and key mapping exist, and Siri's
device-capability representation gained an isPumpkin field.

**Unresolved**: The implementation of _MobileGestalt_get_pumpkinMode has not
been traced to a specific board list, /chosen property, NVRAM variable, IORegistry
node, gestalt cache, platform expert method or entitlement. Nearby NVRAM error
strings in a binary delta do not demonstrate that PumpkinMode uses NVRAM.
Likewise, the HomeAccessory and BreadBoard strings do not establish direct
control-flow edges. The PegasusAPI field could be computed from a product
classification, a request mapping, or a different source.

Required follow-up for a definitive result: obtain lawfully sourced, matching
24B5110a libMobileGestalt Mach-O and relevant SiriInformationSearch binaries;
identify symbol address and ARM64 instructions; follow referenced data tables
and called functions; label IORegistry, sysctl, NVRAM, cache and entitlement
calls; verify on a matching original Apple guest and compare true/false cases.
The repository does not invent disassembly or runtime values.

The original Apple firmware identity is **retained**, never replaced with a
generic iPhone. DeviceTree patching now reads the central JSON. An opt-in
comparison variant adds a synthetic /chosen/PumpkinMode u32=1 property, while
the other variant uses the unchanged Apple ADT. There is **no evidence Apple
MobileGestalt reads this synthetic node**; the generated profile report leaves
the observed MobileGestalt result null. NVRAM values and entitlements are not
forged.

## Product-family comparison

- HomePod: audio-first Apple home accessory platform; not a proven Pumpkin identity.
- Apple TV: tvOS and TV-oriented display/shell; shares home services, but AppleTV
  product identity must not substitute for HomeAccessory17,1.
- HomeAccessory/HomeView: HomeAccessory17,1/J490AP is the actual firmware
  target; its DCP/SGX/touch/audio device tree is distinct from generic iPhone.
- BreadBoard: HomeView launcher family, not evidence of a cosmetic mode.
- TestMode2: appears as an Apple string/type; its relationship to Pumpkin and
  firmware testing is not confirmed.
- Simulator: an Apple Silicon vphone-cli HomeView path exists; its identity
  should not be assumed to function unchanged in Intel qemu-sptm.

## Current boot instrumentation and blockers

The firmware workflow reports profile identity, what ADT properties were
actually injected, per-variant Apple serial logs, observed query/return values
(if any), Siri HomeAccessory/isPumpkin logs (if emitted), services/shells that
have genuine start evidence, and NAND/framebuffer errors. No log match is
reported as **unknown**, never as a successful spoof.

The latest genuine HomeView experiment reaches XNU, APFS and Apple's **restore**
launchd/patchd/ramrod. It does not boot a full installed HomeView system volume.
Missing hardware includes Apple ANS NAND storage, DCP/SGX framebuffer, native
touch/audio/USB services and graphics acceleration. Entitlement checks for
BreadBoard and activation still require a normal userland boot.
